Who grades the grader?

Name
Saeid Asgari
Subject
Judges, rubrics, and agents that learn

Before an agent can improve, something has to tell it whether it did the job. I run experiments on that something: what makes a large language model (LLM) judge strict or lenient, why rubrics drift when a model revises them, and how an agent can learn without a hidden test. These are the write-ups, with the numbers.

  • 2% → 98%October 2026

    Your judge isn’t lenient or strict. Your rubric is.

    Holding the judge and the outputs fixed, what the rubric asks for moved the pass rate on correct outputs from 2% to 98%. Swapping the judge model barely mattered.

    Self-review erases what you taught your rubric

    Four rounds of model self-review pulled two good rubrics toward the reviewer’s own standard. Only delete-only revision was safe.
